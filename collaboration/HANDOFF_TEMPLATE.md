## Integration and continuation

- Proposed shared function-database/status/tool delta (separate from owned files):

- Last proven result:
- Exact next instruction/address/experiment:
- Ready for review, partial handoff, or request to release ownership:

Do not mark a task completed until the coordinator accepts/integrates it. Useful
partial evidence is welcome; do not fill gaps with assumed results.
